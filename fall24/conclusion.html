In general I found that during my second work term at Armour I was able to build upon the technical skills I had developed during my first work term in an environment that put an equal amount of emphasis on the soft skills that would make me more successful in any environment. Through prioritizing my ability to carefully use and mindfully assess my time, as well as the ability to divide and conquer tasks of any size I was able to grow into a more valuable and responsible employee. I also had the ability to spend my time with my work more comfortably and critically assess what parts of my job I did and did not like by learning much more about the software and tools which I was using.
