Coming out of my first work term, where I was a software developer/network technician at Armour Technology Solutions, I gained a solid understanding of what a software development position could look like for me after graduation. Continuing with that position for the fall term, I already had familiarity with my responsibilities and the types of work I would be doing. For this term I focused on being critical in terms of what I did and did not like about my work and using that insight to help guide my future. I also assigned my goals around habitual practices that would make me a better employer in terms of maximizing my efficiency and making myself better at managing the tasks that I am assigned.

This website describes how I focused on making myself into the best employee I could be by working on my time management and organizational skills, and how that affected my working experience.
