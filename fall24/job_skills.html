The hard skills I used during this work term include programming in C#, JS, HTML, and CSS, knowledge of Outlook and inbox rules, knowledge of Azure and Microsoft development tools and environments, knowledge of Visual Studio, and knowledge of Microsoft NAV and SQL databases. The knowledge and skills that I required for this term most frequently came from my first work term at Armour and my schooling. In my first work term I had my first experiences with Visual Studio, C#, and due to that basic knowledge I was able to comfortably expand upon those skills, particularly with Visual Studio and all of its built-in tools for development and debugging, as reflected in my learning goals for this term. For many of the technical skills that I needed for this term and the projects I worked on within it, I developed them as they came up. During my first work I spent a lot of time using AI and documentation to improve my understanding of the tools I was working with, and I continued that in this work term to learn more about NAV, the Azure Document Intelligence Studio, and web services.

Some of the soft skills that I needed the most for this work term include organization and balancing of the tasks I am working on, and time management and setting and meeting deadlines. These skills are similar to the ones I needed and focused on during my first work term, and the techniques I used to improve them largely come from there as well. During my first work term I found myself often working harder than I should have needed to to get a project done by the deadline I set for myself. I also found myself struggling with the limited time I had in a day to get work done, and encountering many errors in my programs that would take me a very long time to fix. I learned the soft skills that helped me the most during this work term from the experiences that I found most difficult about my first work term.
