Work Term Objectives

Learning Goals

The goals I picked for this work term were as follows:

  1. Become better at asking questions about the features and code that I am working on. For example, if I am fixing a bug and the expected behaviour is not stated explicitly or is not very clear, I want to ask questions to clarify it so that I can fix the bug correctly. The same idea applies when working on features.
  2. Become better at managing my time and time-boxing my work by allocating a reasonable amount of time to complete a task or get something working. If I do not finish what I intended within that time box, I will seek help. This way I am not getting stuck on something for a prolonged period of time, while still giving myself a chance to figure things out on my own. I also want to note the story points assigned to user stories and compare those estimates to how long the work actually takes me. If I significantly exceed the estimate, I want to investigate why and understand what slowed me down.
  3. Do a better job of recording pain points and issues when working on different projects, as well as with the various development environments and tools that I use. I want to understand when I am losing time and why it is happening. I also want to make sure I communicate what I find with my co-workers and supervisor when appropriate.
  4. Become a better tester of code, including the code I write. This includes manual testing, testing flows in the mobile app when applicable, testing how the database is being updated, and writing unit tests. It also includes adding unit tests when implementing bug fixes, when it makes sense to do so.
  5. Ensure that I complete the work assigned to me in order from highest to lowest priority. If something new comes up for me to work on or fix, I want to evaluate whether it should take precedence over what I am currently working on. If I am ever unsure about the relative priority of multiple tasks, I will ask my supervisor or another team member for clarification.

Why I picked these goals

I picked these goals because they revolve around developing the soft skills needed to excel as a software developer in industry. Prior to this work term, most of the software development experience I had accumulated related to the hard skills of writing and developing code. I wanted to use this work term as an opportunity to better understand the other skills necessary to work effectively in a software production environment. These include open communication with team members, time management, and the ability to integrate my own work into a large-scale project. More specifically, I wanted to focus on what my team members needed from me in order for me to contribute meaningfully to the project.

Technical focus for the term

Some of the technologies I wanted to work with this term included Visual Studio, SQL Server Management Studio, and SharePoint/TFS. I focused on these technologies because they fall into one of two categories: tools that improve my ability to navigate a large codebase, and tools that improve my communication with the team regarding how my work is progressing.

Database and tooling skills were one of the technical areas I wanted to strengthen during the term.
