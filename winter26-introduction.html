Introduction

One of the fundamental goals of any co-op work term is to gain meaningful experience and a clearer understanding of what it means to work in a professional environment within that field. Following my previous work terms at Armour Technology Solutions, and after learning more about industry practices for software development through my coursework, I was very excited to begin working at Brock Solutions, a company recognized for its work in transportation technology.

This section of the website reflects on my time at Brock Solutions, what I hoped to gain from the experience, the learning goals I set for myself, how effectively I achieved those goals, other things I learned and took away from the term, and examples of the work I completed during the placement.
