Skills Developed

Job Skills

This work term helped me strengthen both technical and transferable skills. On the technical side, I spent most of my time working in the same tools and workflows used by the team day to day. On the transferable side, I continued developing better habits for planning work, communicating blockers, and asking better questions earlier in the process.

Tracking work items, priorities, and bugs was an important part of how I organized my work.

Technical Skills

  • Use Visual Studio to write and debug C#, JavaScript, and AXML code.
  • Use SharePoint and TFS to query boards, review bugs, and sort work by priority and severity.
  • Manage work items by updating tags, statuses, and progress through the board.
  • Create pull requests, respond to review comments, and move changes through approval.
  • Run build scripts, execute regression tests, and document results through comments and bug reports.

Transferable Skills

  • Improve time management, especially through time boxing and more deliberate task planning.
  • Log and communicate pain points earlier instead of waiting until I am fully blocked.
  • Seek clarification sooner when requirements or domain details are uncertain.
  • Reduce rework by surfacing questions earlier and building a stronger understanding of the systems I am working in.
