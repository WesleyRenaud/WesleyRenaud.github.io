Conclusion

My first work term at Brock Solutions gave me a much clearer understanding of what it means to contribute to software development in a professional team environment. A large part of that learning came from understanding how to make sure my work met the expectations of both my teammates and the clients who would ultimately rely on the product.

Beyond the technical skills I used during the term, I learned the importance of clear communication, asking questions at the right time, and discussing best practices with the team. Those conversations helped me see that good software development is not just about making something work, but about contributing to a product that is modular, robust, and secure.

This work term gave me practical experience applying classroom knowledge to a large-scale product.

Looking Forward

In the context of my education as a whole, I am very grateful for the opportunity I was given at Brock to apply the software development experience I have gained through my studies and personal work to a large-scale product with a complex real-world domain, modern architecture, and established development processes. I will carry what I learned from this experience through the rest of my education and into my career.
