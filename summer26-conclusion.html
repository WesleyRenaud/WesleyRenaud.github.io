Conclusion

My second work term at Brock Solutions was defined less by learning the basics of the environment, and more by putting that foundation to use. Coming back to the SmartBag Reconciliation team let me focus on working more independently and making stronger decisions when features and bugs were less straightforward than they first appeared.

Across WSI Speedloader, grouping recovery, and release-cycle work, the term reinforced that contributing well in a large production codebase is about more than completing tickets. It is about making changes that fit existing patterns, understanding customer and client impact, and knowing when to push forward independently versus when to seek advice.

This second term built on my first at Brock by emphasizing independence and stronger judgment.

Looking Forward

I will carry those habits through the rest of my education and into future software roles: investigating thoroughly before committing to a path, ordering work with release risk in mind, and treating communication and careful validation as part of delivering production-ready changes.
