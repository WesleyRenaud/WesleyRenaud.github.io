The goals that I picked for the fall semester were as follows: become better at how I handle working on multiple tasks at once, reduce both the number of and severity of the errors I encounter when programming, learn more CSS styles and attributes, gain a stronger understanding of different project types in Visual Studio, and avoid being overwhelmed by larger or more complex projects.

I picked these goals because they encompass areas of improvement I encountered during my first work term. These goals also blend working on improving my soft skills and my hard skills in a way that will make me more technically proficient in the realm of computer science, while also making me better at time management and organization, which will be beneficial in any education or professional environment that I end up entering.

The technical skills/knowledge that I hoped to gain during this term included learning more CSS, and thus becoming better with web design and creating any form of GUI, and learning more about different types of Visual Studio projects. Visual Studio is the first tool I have used of its type with existing presets available for more easily making different types of programs. Learning what these types represent and how they work would help me understand how these programs are made and help me make them going forward.

The soft/transferable skills that I set myself out to work on revolve around time management skills and my organizational skills in terms of balancing multiple tasks. During my time at university, one of the biggest learning curves I encountered was being able to at any time have a solid grasp of what work I should aim to finish each day in order to stay on track with all of my assessments. Dealing with this in a work environment became more interesting as the time I had to complete each task was more limited as there were only so many hours in a day. During this work term I focused heavily on being able to predict how long a certain task would take me, and also minimizing that amount of time. My goal was to break down every task into smaller tasks where it was easier to see how long they would take to complete, and break down my days so I had a strong vision of how much time I would have to work on any projects I had open at the time, and be able to allocate that time accordingly. Being able to divide my work and time like this will teach me how to be efficient and wise with my time, making it easier to complete tasks in any educational or work setting.
