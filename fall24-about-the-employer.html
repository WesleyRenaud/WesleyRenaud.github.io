During the fall 2024 term I continued my position from the summer 2024 term where I worked for a company named Armour Technology Solutions. Armour Technology Solutions, or Armour, is a small tech company based in Barrie, Ontario.

Armour Technology Services Logo.


Amour Technology Solutions has two lines of business: custom software development and networking/support. Armour prioritizes creating strong relationships with their customers, and as a result, most of the work that armour does is with clients in which they have pre- existing relations. Work in the custom software development branch may consist of creating a brand new program for a client, adding new features to an existing base of code, or troubleshooting errors in an existing project. I worked in the custom software development branch. The networking/support branch has the employees performing various networking tests, troubleshooting issues with servers, moving a client to some new piece of software, performing on-site visits to install hardware or perform some maintenance task, and much more.
