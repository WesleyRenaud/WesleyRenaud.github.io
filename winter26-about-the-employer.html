About the Employer

For my Winter 2026 work term, I worked at Brock Solutions. Brock Solutions develops software and hardware solutions across several industries, with a particularly strong presence in transportation systems such as airlines and airports. The company is divided into business units that focus on different products and domains. I was part of the SmartSuite business unit, which builds software tools used by airlines and airports to manage flight activity, baggage, cargo, loading containers, and other operational processes.

Brock Solutions logo.


The SmartSuite mobile app is one of the main products used within this part of the company. It is designed to support airport operations by tracking activity related to flights, baggage, containers, and loading processes. Users can review flight statuses, arrival and departure information, associated containers, and baggage activity. The web application supports many of the same workflows while also providing more detailed interfaces for management tasks and report generation.


The SmartSuite business unit also includes several connected systems that support the overall platform. These include tools for weight and balance planning, baggage tracking, and passenger processing. I worked on the SmartBag side of the product, specifically with a team focused on baggage reconciliation. That work involves helping ensure that the right bags are associated with the right flights and that issues are surfaced clearly to airport staff when action is required.

Baggage handling is one of the airport workflows supported by SmartSuite tools.
