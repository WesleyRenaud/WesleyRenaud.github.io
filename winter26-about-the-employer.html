Company Context

What Brock Solutions builds

Brock Solutions works on software and hardware solutions across several industries, with a strong focus on transportation and automation. The company is organized into business units so teams can specialize in the systems, clients, and operational problems that matter most within each domain.

My Area

The SmartSuite business unit

SmartSuite builds web and mobile tools used by airlines and airports to monitor flights, manage containers, track baggage and cargo, and support staff carrying out time-sensitive work on the ground. That made it a strong environment for learning how software supports real operational processes rather than isolated technical tasks.

Baggage handling is one of the airport workflows supported by SmartSuite tools.

Operational Focus

Software tied directly to airport workflows

The SmartSuite mobile app supports airport activity by helping users track flight status, baggage movement, loading activity, and container information. The related web application supports many of the same workflows while also providing more detailed management views and reporting features.

The business unit includes several connected systems that support the larger platform. These systems handle work such as weight and balance planning, baggage tracking, and passenger processing. My team worked on the SmartBag side of the product, specifically in baggage reconciliation, where software helps determine whether bags are correctly associated with a flight and whether staff need to take action.

SmartLoad

Supports load planning and weight-and-balance workflows for flights.

SmartBag

Tracks baggage activity and supports reconciliation before loading.

SmartClear

Supports passenger and baggage movement through airport checkpoints.
