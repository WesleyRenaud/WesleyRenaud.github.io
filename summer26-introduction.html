Introduction

One of the fundamental goals of any co-op work term is to gain meaningful experience and a clearer understanding of what it means to work in a professional environment within that field. After completing my Winter 2026 work term at Brock Solutions, I returned for a second term in Summer 2026. Continuing with the same company and product area gave me the chance to build on the domain knowledge, development practices, and team workflows I developed during the previous term.

My main goal going into this second term was to gain more independence. During my first term at Brock, I built a basic familiarity with the tools and processes used on the SmartBag Reconciliation team. That foundation meant that in Summer 2026 I could focus less on learning the environment from scratch, and more on using what I already knew to handle assigned features more independently: investigating how to implement something myself, deciding the order of tickets, and owning more of the path from work item to completed change.

I also wanted to improve how I approached bugs. Beyond fixing the immediate issue, that meant investigating multiple possible solutions and getting better at evaluating whether something was as-designed, a configuration problem, a user issue, or a genuine defect. Those habits mattered especially during release cycles, when time-sensitive bugs required careful judgment as much as quick coding.

This section of the website reflects on my Summer 2026 time at Brock Solutions, what I hoped to gain from the experience, the learning goals I set for myself, how effectively I achieved those goals, other things I learned and took away from the term, and examples of the work I completed during the placement.
